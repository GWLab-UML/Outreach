# Oyster Tissue Scavenger Hunt 

- Brief description
- Students will look at the pre-made slides of oyster histology under the microscopes and look around the slide to find the picked-out tissue and any other tissues of interest. 
- **Target Age Group:**
- 12-14

## Learning Objectives
For students to learn and gain knowledge on the anatomy of the eastern oyster, while learning to use a compound microscope and how to appropriately look at histology slides.

## Materials 
- Compound microscope
- pre-made histology slides
- kemwipes
- Pencils/Pens
- Shucking knife
- Shucking glove
- tweezers
- gloves
- plastic bags (waste)
- weigh boats
- "The Eastern Oyster" textbook for reference for tissues.
- (If time: live oysters to be shucked at the end)

## Activities 
1. Set up the microscope, pass out paper and pencils, and have pre-made slides ready for each station (depending on the size of the group, kids might need to be in groups)
2. Start off with a basic lesson on how to use the microscope with care and what each magnification means.
3. Have the kids point out what type of tissue they see and answer any questions.
4. The kids should fill out the worksheet, finding tissues that correlate with the worksheet and premade slides.
5. When the kids are done, if you have a prize for the group that found the most, this can be given out.
6. If time, shuck the oysters; you have to show the kids the organs and the areas they were just looking at.
7. Clean up and place oysters in plastic bags to be disposed of, and place slides back into slide holder cases.
