# Flip 7: Marine Edition

-   Learning about species diversity and abundance of the North Shore region.
-   **Target Age Group: Teens**

## Learning Objectives

-   The goal of this activity is to help students learn about the local fauna and algae in preparation of the field trip scheduled with the Boys and Girls Club at Greater Lowell.

## Materials

-   Playing cards of marine animals and algae created by lab members

## Activities

1.  There are cards with the common and scientific name of a marine invertebrates or algae.
    -   Each card has a corresponding number (1-13) with it that indicates the abundance of that species.
    -   Each number card has a quantity in the deck equal to its number (one 1, two 2s, three 3s, up to thirteen 13s).
    -   The higher the number, the greater the likelihood of seeing that species in the wild and vice versa.
2.  At the start of every round, there is a dealer that asks the players if they want to pull a card.
3.  If a player does not want to pull a card, their turn ends. They sum up the total of their cards.
4.  If a player does want to pull a card, it has to be a unique card.
    -   If the card is unique, they can continue playing.
    -   If the play does get a duplicate, they bust.
        -   They are out for that round and get zero points.
5.  The round concludes when players either stops their turn, busts, or reaches seven unique number cards.
    -   If a player reaches seven unique cards, they sum up all their points and get an additional fifteen points.
6.  There are three unique action cards. These cards can only be used during that round.
    -   +2: Player gets an additional two points added if they do not bust.
    -   Freeze: Player can freeze themselves or another player. When player gets frozen, their turn ends for that round and must sum up their points and adds it to their total.
    -   Second Chance: If a player gets a duplicate card, they can use their second chance card to discard that duplicate card. Players can only use the second chance card once.
7.  There are multiple rounds. The game ends when a player reaches 200 total points from all rounds.
