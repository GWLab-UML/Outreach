# Candy DNA

- Students will learn about the makeup of DNA using candy as a model!
- **Target Age Group:** teens

## Reference Links
- https://www.sciencebuddies.org/stem-activities/candy-DNA-model

## Learning Objectives
- learn what DNA is and what it's made up
- understand why DNA is a double helix
- understand nucleotides and their pairing
- learn how DNA replicates via a template strand

## Materials 
- twizzlers (or other rope-like candy)
- soft candy that comes in 4 different colors (like gummy bears)
- toothpicks
- paper
- writing utensils
- whiteboard and expo markers if available!

## Activities 
1. Introduce DNA
	- Who's heard of DNA? What is it? What does it look like?
	- Have students draw what it looks like on the whiteboard
2. Introduce DNA code
	- DNA is made up of nucleotides (A, T, G, and C). They pair together in very specific ways:
		- As with Ts
		- Gs with Cs
3. Start on the whiteboard - add nucleotides to one strand, and as a group work through what nucleotides should be on the other strand
	- we're going to build on this with candy DNA!
		- each kid will get 2 twizzlers (strands of DNA) and 4 different colored gummy bears
4. Distribute candy and start building DNA!
	- on a piece of paper, each kid will separate out the gummy bear colors into 4 groups based on color and write down which nucleotide is which (e.g. green is A) 
	- they'll "bind" two paired nucleotides together with toothpicks, and attach them to the twizzlers - see image below!
	![[Screenshot 2026-04-30 at 10.46.12 AM.png]]
	- they'll put their candy DNA onto a piece of paper to transcribe the code
	- they can also twist it to make a double helix!
	![[Screenshot 2026-04-30 at 10.49.00 AM.png]]
5. if time remains, kids can then replicate their DNA - instruct them to split their candy DNA in half, so that each half has a twizzler with gummy bears
6. Using their template strand and code, they will add more gummy bears to end with 2 double-stranded DNA![[Screenshot 2026-05-05 at 11.12.23 AM.png]]
