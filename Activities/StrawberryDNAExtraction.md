# Strawberry DNA Extraction

Extracting DNA from strawberries to describe what DNA is made of and demonstarte that it can be extracted.
- **Target Age Group:** Teens

## Reference links
- [Strawberry DNA Extraction](https://www.genome.gov/about-genomics/teaching-tools/strawberry-dna-extraction)

## Materials:
- Resealable plastic bags
- Carton of strawberries (2x strawberries per individual)
- Dish detergent
- Salt
- Jug of water
- Plastic cups
- Coffee filters
- Cold rubbing alcohol
- Spoons
- Trays
- Paper towels
- Gloves
- Phone/other timer device
## Activity 
1. Remove green tops of strawberries. Place strawberries in a plastic bag. After closing the bag, SMASH the strawberries for ~2 minutes until nice and smashed!
2. Make DNA extraction solution by adding the following to the first plastic cup:
  a. 2 teaspoon dish detergent
  b. 1 teaspoon salt
  c. 1/2 cup water
3. After gently mixing the extraction solution (until salt is dissolved), pour it into the bag of smashed strawberries.
4. Reseal the bag and *gently* massage and mix the mixture for a minute. Try to avoid making too many soap bubbles! 
5. Place the coffee filter over the opening of the second plastic cup. open the plastic bag, and pour the liquid onto the coffee filter. Then wait for the liquid to drip through the coffee filter into the plastic cup, leaving the strawberry *schmutz* on the coffee filter. 
6. You’re almost there! Pour an equal amount of cold rubbing alcohol into the liquid in the second plastic cup. 
7. Gently move the cup around in circles and watch the whitish *snot-like* material form in the upper layer of liquid — that is the strawberry DNA!
8. Use the coffee stirrer to pick up strawberry DNA out of the liquid.
9. If need additional material and there is too much time left: talk about DNA and/or repeat the above steps with cherries (which are harder to extract DNA from).
